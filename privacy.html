Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who we are

Skin Vault ("the app", "we") is operated by Filip Duda. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and what choices you have. Questions: fifik.dudik@gmail.com.

Age requirement (13+)

Skin Vault is not for children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account or use the app (older where your country sets a higher minimum age for online services). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact fifik.dudik@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data promptly.

Data we collect

Account data: your email address, a sign-in identifier from Google or Steam if you use them (for Steam: your SteamID), your profile name/picture supplied by those services, and the nickname you choose.

Portfolio data you enter: skins you own, purchase price and date, and the value history of your portfolio.

Preferences stored on your device: theme colors, layout, language, and cached prices.

Purchase status: whether you have a Skin Vault Pro subscription (handled through the app store and RevenueCat; we never see your payment card).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics trackers.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your portfolio and its value over time, calculate profit and loss, show the leaderboard, and provide Pro features. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Leaderboard

By default you appear on the leaderboard: other signed-in users can see your nickname, profile picture, your portfolio's total value and your five most valuable skins. You can hide yourself at any time in Profile → "Show me on the leaderboard". Your email address is never shown.

Services that process data for us

Supabase (database and authentication), Google and Steam (sign-in), RevenueCat with Apple/Google (subscriptions), and image hosts (Steam CDN, wsrv.nl) that deliver skin pictures — those hosts see your IP address when a picture loads. Skin prices come from third-party market data providers and contain no personal data. Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms.

Where data is stored & security

Data is stored with our database provider and may be processed outside your country. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to each user's portfolio is restricted so that only that user can read or change it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention & deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete everything yourself at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account permanently removes your account, portfolio, history and profile. You can also clear only your chart history or reset your portfolio there. Or write to fifik.dudik@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact fifik.dudik@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above changes when we do, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
