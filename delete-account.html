Delete your Skin Vault account

Skin Vault · by Filip Duda

Delete your account in the app

Open Skin Vault and sign in. Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account and all of its data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Or ask us by e-mail

If you can no longer use the app, write to fifik.dudik@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account (or tell us your Steam profile if you signed in with Steam) with the subject "Delete my Skin Vault account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your login, profile (nickname, picture, Steam link), all skins in your portfolio, your sales, your portfolio value history and your place on the leaderboard. Settings saved on your phone are removed when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

Nothing personal is kept by Skin Vault. If you bought Skin Vault Pro, the store (Google Play / Apple) and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their privacy policies, and a subscription is cancelled in your store account, not by deleting the Skin Vault account. Skin market prices are not personal data and are not affected.

Only want to remove some data?

In Profile you can also clear only your chart history, reset your portfolio, or hide yourself from the leaderboard without deleting the account.
